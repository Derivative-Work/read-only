A crane shot at night in steady rain, rising from an old payphone on a wet San Francisco Tenderloin street corner up over the block. Every shop's metal shutter is rolled down; in every doorway stands a still robot doorman in a dark suit with a gold tie and a thin glowing slit for eyes. Above, old apartment hotels with bay windows and fire escapes, every window lit, a small red light glowing in each one. Glowing red neon signs over the shutters. Parked cars along the wet street reflecting white LED streetlights and neon, rain streaking through the light. Near the end the camera whips around fast. Photorealistic cinematic night footage shot on 35mm film, natural motion.
