Inside a small Filipino eatery late at night after closing, empty and still, the camera moving slowly in over a table by the front window. The sound is the quiet room: steady rain falling outside on the wet street and pattering on the window glass and the half-lowered metal shutter, a refrigerator humming behind the counter, the faint electric buzz of a neon sign outside, now and then a car passing slowly on the wet road in the distance. No people, no speech, no music. Natural, documentary room sound, close and quiet.
