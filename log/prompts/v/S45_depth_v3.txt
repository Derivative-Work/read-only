A static night shot from the wet sidewalk into the open doorway of a small old yarn shop. A stocky man stands a few steps inside the shop, behind the threshold, as a solid black silhouette against the warm light at the back of the shop, perfectly still except for a slow breath and a slight tilt of his head; his front stays in total shadow. Rain falls through the light spilling onto the wet sidewalk and drips from the door frame. At the very end, the shop's metal roll-down shutter slams down fast across the shopfront, between him and the camera, and hides him. Photorealistic cinematic night footage shot on 35mm film, natural subtle motion.
