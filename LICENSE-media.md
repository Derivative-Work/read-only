## What this covers

- The video in every cut: the main video, the vertical cut, the animatic and the drafts.
- The music: the Suno V6 track "Read-Only", its stems, the post-filtered master and the bounce under the end card.
- The lyrics, captions (`deliverables/*.srt`) and thumbnail.
- Generated images and clips: the FLUX.2 [dev] stills and keyframes, the LTX-2.3 and LatentSync takes, the props
  under `assets/gen/`, and the rendered frames, overlays and stills under `renders/`, `takes/` and `archive/`.
- The production record, written by the agent: the brief's mirror, logs, prompts, audits, shot list, timing map, upload
  metadata, and the other documents under `docs/`, `log/`, `style/`, `shots/`, `timing/` and `lyrics/`.

## What this doesn't cover

- **Code** (`scripts/`, `scenes/`, `overlays/`, `config/`, `workflows/` and the build files): MIT, see
  [LICENSE](LICENSE). Where an overlay file holds both code and on-screen text or design, the code is MIT; the frames
  it renders are CC BY-NC-SA.
- **Fonts** (`assets/fonts/`): Inter, JetBrains Mono, Oooh Baby and VT323 under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, and
  Permanent Marker under the Apache License 2.0, each by its authors (the licence file beside each font).
- **Textures and models** (`assets/textures/`, `assets/models/`): CC0 from Poly Haven, not in this repository.
  `sources.tsv` in each folder gives every file's URL and hash, and the fetch scripts re-download them.
- **The breakfast photo** (S01b, on the phone's screen): the creator's own photo from March 2018, the video's only real
  image. It appears in the video under this licence as part of the video; the photo file itself is not in this
  repository and is not separately licensed.
- **The human's reference material** (`reference/private/`, never published) and **other publishers' pages** saved
  for the fact check (`reference/sources/`, never published; the `.tsv` files beside them give their URLs).
