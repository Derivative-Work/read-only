## The eatery (S01a–S01d, S14, S49), from the photos and the human's note

The human: "it is *extremely* informal, a hole-in-the-wall, with a cafeteria-style layout of food (a Filipino would
call it a 'turo-turo'), and a line of tables. It is as clean as one might expect of the area without being
out-of-place, and very much not polished."

What to take (generic traits, not the real business):
- **Front:** a narrow single storefront in an old grey masonry building, a barred transom over the window, a cramped
  door to one side, a backlit box sign above (ours reads "r/Philippines" in hand-painted letters). Neighbours have
  roll-down security gates.
- **Inside:** turo-turo: point-at-the-food steam table of hotel pans behind a sneeze guard; a hand-painted
  chalkboard menu above the counter with numbered photo cards of dishes under it; green cafeteria trays; a small
  drinks price sign; stainless kitchen wall behind.
- **Seating:** one line of plain wooden tables against a mustard-yellow wall, black ladder-back chairs, terracotta
  tile floor, a string of paper star garland. Fluorescent light.
- **The plate (S01b):** black textured tray, white paper plate, plastic fork and spoon, paper napkin, paper cup.
  Fried fish, fried egg and garlic rice (a silog breakfast).
- **Tone:** worn and lived-in, clean but not polished: scuffs, taped signs, mismatched fixtures. Not grimy.

What never to copy: the real name, sign, phone number, menu wording, logo, house-shaped sign, or anyone who works
there (brief, "Content guardrails").

## The visitor badge and schedule (S01c, S38–S39, S46, S49)

From the scan: the badge is a printed adhesive label with a photo, the visitor's name, and the check-in time
("Mar 27, 2018 · 10:51 AM"). The schedule is one printed page: a greeting, eight time slots from 11:00 AM to 4:30 PM
(with a lunch slot), a room line and "Good luck!". The rendered props keep the layout and the real times, put the
names behind redaction bars, and have no logo, mascot or room name. The badge reads "VISITOR" and the date.
