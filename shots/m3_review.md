# M3 review: the grey-box animatic

Watch `renders/m3/m3_animatic_v2.mp4` (640×360, 24 fps, with the post-filtered master). The anchor stills, one a third
of the way into each shot, are on `renders/m3/m3_sheet_{a,b,c}.jpg`. The gate is the brief's: **approve framing and
timing**. Look, light and texture come at M4.

## How to read it

- **Top right:** the shot ID and its pipeline code. **Bottom:** the lyric line, each word lighting as it's sung.
  **Top left, in brackets:** the overlay or edit effect the brief plans for that shot (JS, F, Real, LS), which is
  drawn at M4–M6, not here. Act 2 shots carry "[Act 2: made photoreal at M5]".
- **Stand-ins:**
  - the Dev: teal hoodie, brown apron, tool belt;
  - the CEO: grey hood, white cape with a red collar;
  - the mods: orange MOD vests and brooms;
  - the VIP bots: dark boxy robots with gold badges and briefcases;
  - the good bots: small pale robots with lanterns;
  - the hype man: a black silhouette.

  Mouths open with the lead vocal stem's loudness in the B+M shots.
- **The light:** sunny morning in the cold open, night from the S01e cut. The block goes dark at S15. The corner lamp
  flickers back on at S17 and the street lamps follow; the upper windows relight from S18.
