## On "blackmail"

The sung line ("Said 'blackmail' on the line… I got the tape") is the Dev's account. On a June 7, 2023 call with
moderators, Reddit's CEO said Apollo's developer had "threatened" Reddit and was "coercing" it. "Blackmail" was the
developer's description of what the CEO told employees. The developer had recorded his own call with Reddit (legal in
Canada) and released it. On that recording, the Reddit employee calls his reading of the remark "a complete
misinterpretation" and apologizes. The lobby screen in the video (S10) shows the words the CEO actually used:
"HE'S THREATENING US". Source: [TechCrunch, Jun 9 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/06/09/reddit-ceo-doubles-down-on-attack-on-apollo-developer-in-drama-filled-ama/).
